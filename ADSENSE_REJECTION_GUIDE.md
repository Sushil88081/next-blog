# AdSense Rejection - What to Do Next

## ⏰ When Can You Resubmit?

**Minimum Wait Time:** 7 days  
**Recommended Wait Time:** 14-30 days  
**Best Practice:** Wait 30 days and make substantial improvements

### Why Wait?

- Google needs time to re-crawl your site
- You need time to fix the issues
- Making substantial improvements increases approval chances
- Rushing resubmission often leads to another rejection

## 🔍 Step 1: Check the Rejection Reason (CRITICAL!)

**This is the most important step!**

1. **Sign in to AdSense**
   - Go to https://www.google.com/adsense
   - Sign in with your account

2. **Go to Policy Center**
   - Click "Policy center" in the left menu
   - Or go to "Sites" → Click your site → View rejection reason

3. **Read the Specific Reason**
   - Google will tell you EXACTLY why it was rejected
   - Common reasons include:
     - "Not enough content"
     - "Low value content"
     - "Navigation issues"
     - "Missing required pages"
     - "Policy violations"
     - "Site not ready"

4. **Take Notes**
   - Write down the exact rejection reason
   - Note any specific pages mentioned
   - Save any error messages

## 📋 Step 2: Fix the Issues

Based on the rejection reason, fix the problems:

### If Rejected for "Not Enough Content"
- ✅ You have 53+ posts (should be enough)
- **Action:** Add 10-20 more high-quality posts
- **Focus:** Each post should be 500+ words
- **Wait:** 14-30 days while adding content

### If Rejected for "Low Value Content"
- **Action:** Improve existing posts
- Add more detailed explanations
- Include code examples
- Add images/screenshots
- Make tutorials more comprehensive
- **Wait:** 14-30 days while improving content

### If Rejected for "Navigation Issues"
- **Action:** Check all navigation links
- Ensure menu works on mobile
- Fix any broken links
- Add breadcrumbs if missing
- **Wait:** 7-14 days after fixes

### If Rejected for "Missing Required Pages"
- **Action:** Verify all pages exist:
  - ✅ Home page
  - ✅ About page
  - ✅ Contact page
  - ✅ Privacy Policy
  - ✅ Terms of Service (if mentioned)
- **Wait:** 7-14 days after adding pages

### If Rejected for "Site Not Ready"
- **Action:** Check:
  - Is site fully functional?
  - Are all pages loading?
  - Is there placeholder content?
  - Are there "Coming Soon" pages?
- **Wait:** 14-30 days after making site complete

### If Rejected for "Policy Violations"
- **Action:** Review AdSense policies
- Remove any violating content
- Fix copyright issues
- Remove adult/illegal content
- **Wait:** 30 days after fixing violations

## 📅 Step 4: Resubmission Timeline

### Option 1: Quick Resubmission (7-14 days)
- **When:** If rejection was for minor issues (navigation, missing page)
- **Action:** Fix the specific issue, wait 7-14 days
- **Risk:** Higher chance of rejection if issues not fully fixed

### Option 2: Standard Resubmission (14-30 days) ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **When:** Most rejection reasons
- **Action:** Fix issues + add 10-20 new posts + improve existing content
- **Risk:** Lower, if you make substantial improvements

### Option 3: Extended Resubmission (30-60 days)
- **When:** Policy violations, low content quality, major issues
- **Action:** Major site overhaul, 30+ new posts, significant improvements
- **Risk:** Lowest, best chance of approval

## 🚀 Step 6: Resubmit

After waiting and making improvements:

1. **Sign in to AdSense**
   - Go to https://www.google.com/adsense

2. **Go to Sites**
   - Click "Sites" in left menu
   - Find your site

3. **Check Status**
   - Should show "Needs attention" or "Requires review"
   - Verify ads.txt shows "Authorized"

4. **Request Review**
   - Click on your site
   - Confirm AdSense code is placed
   - Click "Next"
   - Click "Request review"

5. **Wait for Review**
   - Review takes 2-14 days typically
   - Can take up to 4 weeks
   - You'll receive email notification

- ❌ **Don't submit multiple times** - Wait for result first
- ❌ **Don't ignore rejection reason** - Fix the specific issue
- ❌ **Don't make minor changes** - Make substantial improvements
